# Movie Community System - Product Overview

## Project Purpose
A comprehensive full-stack movie community platform that enables users to discover, track, review, and discuss movies while building social connections with fellow movie enthusiasts.

## Core Value Proposition
- **Movie Discovery**: Browse and filter movies by genre, year, and rating
- **Personal Movie Management**: Track movies with watchlist (to-watch, watching, completed) and progress tracking
- **Social Community**: Connect with friends, share reviews, and engage in movie discussions
- **Comprehensive Reviews**: Write detailed movie reviews with spoiler protection
- **Real-time Notifications**: Stay updated on friend activities and community interactions

## Key Features & Capabilities

### User Management
- Secure user registration and authentication with JWT tokens
- Profile management with customizable settings
- Password and email change functionality
- User dashboard with personalized statistics

### Movie Features
- Comprehensive movie database with detailed information
- Advanced filtering by genre, year, and rating
- Movie details pages with reviews and ratings
- Watchlist management with three status levels and progress tracking

### Social Features
- Friend system with request/accept/decline functionality
- Direct messaging between users
- Movie reviews with spoiler tags and helpful voting
- Notification system for social interactions

### Administrative Features
- Complete admin panel for user and content management
- Movie and genre management
- Content moderation tools
- System audit trails and reporting

## Target Users

### Primary Users
- **Movie Enthusiasts**: Users who want to track their movie watching progress and discover new films
- **Social Movie Watchers**: Users who enjoy discussing movies and connecting with like-minded individuals
- **Review Writers**: Users who want to share detailed movie opinions and read others' perspectives

### Secondary Users
- **Casual Browsers**: Users who occasionally browse for movie recommendations
- **Community Moderators**: Administrators managing content and user interactions

## Use Cases

### Personal Movie Management
- Track movies across different viewing stages (to-watch, watching, completed)
- Monitor viewing progress with percentage completion
- Maintain organized watchlists for future viewing

### Social Interaction
- Connect with friends who share similar movie interests
- Exchange movie recommendations through direct messaging
- Participate in community discussions through reviews and comments

### Content Discovery
- Discover new movies through genre and rating filters
- Read community reviews before watching
- Get personalized recommendations based on viewing history

### Community Building
- Build networks of movie enthusiast friends
- Share movie experiences and opinions
- Participate in movie-related events and discussions
